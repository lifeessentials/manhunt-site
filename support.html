Email support@manhunt.app for account help, TestFlight access, or to report a problem in a game.

In the app

  • Settings → Privacy deletes GPS tracks and the optional fitness score.
  • Settings → Sign-in deletes your account.
  • Settings → Permissions opens system location, camera, and Health controls.

Location not updating

Grant Always location, not While Using, and keep battery optimisation off for Manhunt on Android. Sharing stops when the hunt ends.
