Manhunt is a running game played on real streets. You use it at your own risk. Look up, stay off roads, and stop if you need to. Do not trespass, and follow local laws.

Accounts

You can play as a guest on one phone or create an email account. You are responsible for the name you choose and for anyone you invite into a game.

Location

By joining a game you agree that other players in that game can see your location while the hunt is running, including when the phone is locked if you grant Always location.

Fair play

Do not spoof your location, harass other players, or use the app to follow someone who is not in your game. We may remove accounts that break these rules.
