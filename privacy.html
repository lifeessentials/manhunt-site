Privacy policy

Privacy Terms Support

Manhunt is a live outdoor game. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. The app is operated from the Manhunt project. Contact support@manhunt.app.

What we collect

  • Account data: email if you create an account, display name, colour, and a guest identifier if you play without email.
  • Precise location while you are in a lobby or an active hunt, so other players in that game can see you on the map according to the game rules.
  • GPS tracks for finished games, used for replay and stats.
  • Push tokens so we can send catch, start, and out-of-bounds alerts.
  • Optional Health data if you turn it on: recent workouts, resting heart rate, and VO2 max, used only to seed a private fitness score. Raw heart-rate samples stay on your phone.
  • Crash reports via Sentry when crash reporting is enabled, including device and app version.

Location sharing

Precise location is shared with other players in your game while a hunt is running. Hunters usually see a blurred circle rather than an exact pin. Sharing stops when the game ends. Live map pins are dropped 24 hours after a match.

How long we keep data

  • GPS tracks are deleted 30 days after a game ends, or sooner if you delete them in Settings → Privacy.
  • Live locations are removed 24 hours after a match.
  • Account data stays until you delete your account in Settings → Sign-in.

Your choices

You can play as a guest, delete your tracks, turn Health off, delete your fitness score, or delete your account from the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, tracks, and fitness score from our servers.

Children

Manhunt is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13.
